Kafka- Real time event streaming platform
Acts like a central pipeline

App--> Kafka--> Consumers

i. Distributed --means data is distributed across different machines 
ii. Supports horizontal scaling-- means if traffic gets increased then we can increase or add the machines or nodes or broker
iii. Fault tolerance-- means if one broker is lost then data will remain in other cause it maintain replicas
iv. Kafka Cluster

Kafka and streaming--Data is generated and processed immediately e.g. payment

Kafka Architecture
1. producer- one who sends the data to kafka
2. Topic- logical channel(like category)
3. Consumer- one who reads the data from kafka

Messaging models
1. Publish subscribe-pub-sub
2. Point-to-point
